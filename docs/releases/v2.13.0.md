CLIO Kit 2.13.0 adds the servers behind CLIO 0.9.8's chemistry, ocean and weather, geospatial, electronics and home-automation work.

New servers: ase, avogadro, decimer, fdmnes, graspa and pubchem for computational chemistry; ocean, weather, copernicus, earthdata and grids for ocean and weather science; geoservices and geoprocess for ArcGIS, OGC and STAC access and geospatial operations; spice for circuit simulation; slicer for 3D printing; pdf; and home-assistant. geo 2.4.0 draws layers on an interactive globe, and the plain globe server is removed.

See the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/iowarp/clio-kit/blob/v2.13.0/CHANGELOG.md).
