# Material Science Research Domains

## Structural Materials
**Focus**: Mechanical properties, deformation, failure, lifetime
**Sub-fields**:
- Metals & alloys (steels, aluminum, titanium, nickel superalloys)
- High-entropy alloys (HEAs) / Multi-principal element alloys (MPEAs)
- Ceramics and ceramic composites
- Intermetallics
- Refractory materials (W, Mo, Ta for extreme environments)
- Fatigue, fracture mechanics, creep
- Additive manufacturing of structural parts

**Key phenomena**: Dislocation motion, grain boundary effects, phase transformations, precipitate hardening, fatigue crack propagation

**Common methods**: Tensile/compression testing, fatigue testing, TEM/EBSD, XRD, molecular dynamics, DFT for defect energetics, crystal plasticity FEM

---

## Functional Materials
**Focus**: Electronic, magnetic, optical, or thermal function
**Sub-fields**:
- Semiconductors (Si, GaN, perovskites)
- Ferroelectrics and piezoelectrics
- Magnetic materials (hard/soft magnets, spintronics)
- Thermoelectrics
- Superconductors
- Topological materials

**Key phenomena**: Band structure, carrier mobility, polarization switching, magnetization reversal, Seebeck coefficient, Cooper pairs

**Common methods**: Hall effect, impedance spectroscopy, SQUID magnetometry, DFT band structure, phonon calculations

---

## Energy Materials
**Focus**: Energy storage, conversion, and efficiency
**Sub-fields**:
- Lithium-ion and next-gen batteries (solid-state, Na-ion, Li-S)
- Fuel cells (PEMFC, SOFC)
- Solar cells (perovskite, Si, CIGS)
- Hydrogen storage materials
- Supercapacitors
- Nuclear materials

**Key phenomena**: Ion transport, electrochemical reactions, degradation mechanisms, dendrite formation, phase stability under cycling

**Common methods**: Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy, cyclic voltammetry, in-situ XRD, DFT for voltage profiles, CALPHAD

---

## Biomaterials
**Focus**: Materials interfacing with biological systems
**Sub-fields**:
- Bone scaffolds and orthopedic implants
- Dental materials
- Cardiovascular devices
- Drug delivery systems
- Tissue engineering matrices
- Biodegradable polymers

**Key phenomena**: Biocompatibility, osseointegration, biodegradation, cell adhesion, protein adsorption

**Common methods**: Cell viability assays, in vivo implantation, SEM/TEM, mechanical testing (physiological conditions), ISO standards testing

---

## Nanomaterials
**Focus**: Size-dependent properties at 1–100 nm scale
**Sub-fields**:
- Nanoparticles (metal, oxide, quantum dots)
- Carbon nanomaterials (graphene, CNTs, fullerenes)
- 2D materials (MoS₂, h-BN, TMDs)
- Nanocomposites
- Nanowires and nanostructured films

**Key phenomena**: Quantum confinement, surface-to-volume ratio effects, plasmonic resonance, van der Waals interactions

**Common methods**: TEM/HRTEM, SAXS, Raman spectroscopy, DFT for 2D materials, AIMD

---

## Polymers & Soft Matter
**Focus**: Organic macromolecules and complex fluids
**Sub-fields**:
- Structural polymers (PEEK, epoxy, UHMWPE)
- Hydrogels and elastomers
- Block copolymers
- Polymer composites (CFRP, GFRP)
- Conducting polymers

**Key phenomena**: Chain mobility, glass transition, viscoelasticity, cross-linking, self-assembly

**Common methods**: DSC, DMA, rheology, SAXS/WAXS, coarse-grained MD

---

## Computational Materials Science
**Focus**: Simulation and prediction of material properties
**Sub-fields**:
- Density functional theory (DFT)
- Molecular dynamics (MD)
- Phase-field modeling
- CALPHAD / thermodynamic modeling
- Machine learning interatomic potentials
- High-throughput screening

**Key phenomena**: Electronic structure, interatomic forces, thermodynamic stability, kinetics of phase transformations

**Common methods**: VASP, Quantum ESPRESSO, LAMMPS, Thermo-Calc, Materials Project API, AFLOW

---

## Characterization Methods Focus
**Focus**: Development or application of characterization techniques
**Sub-fields**:
- Electron microscopy (SEM, TEM, STEM-EDS, EELS)
- X-ray techniques (XRD, XPS, SAXS, synchrotron)
- Atom probe tomography (APT)
- Neutron scattering
- In-situ and operando methods
- Correlative characterization

---

## Research Maturity Levels

| Level | Description | Typical Output |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| Exploratory | New phenomenon, first observation | Letter, short communication |
| Mechanistic | Understanding why | Full article, 2–4 experiments |
| Optimization | Making it better | Engineering paper, design rules |
| Application | Real device/system | Applied paper, patent |
| Review/synthesis | Summarizing field | Review article, perspective |
